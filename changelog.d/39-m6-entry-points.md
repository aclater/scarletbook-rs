Deprecated
- **One reading entry point per concept.** The range and stored-frame entry points below are deprecated in 0.6.0 and **will be removed before 1.0, which will break callers then**. They still work and give the same frames, errors and log lines; each call only warns. `Disc::{track_audio, range_audio, track_frames, range_frames, stored_frame_list}`, `Disc::{read_track_audio, read_frames}`, `into_cursor()` and `Disc::continue_*` stay, and `Disc::area_frames` stays as the diagnostic form. No C ABI changed (#39, #000). Migration:
  - **`Disc`'s cursor twins:** before, `disc.track_audio_cursor(slot, n)?` and `disc.range_audio_cursor(slot, first, end)?`. After, `disc.audio_cursor(slot, FrameSpan::Track(n), CursorOptions::new())?` and `disc.audio_cursor(slot, FrameSpan::Range(first, end), CursorOptions::new())?`, or with `&mut disc`, `disc.track_audio(slot, n)?.into_cursor()` and `disc.range_audio(slot, first, end)?.into_cursor()`.
  - **`Disc`'s stored-frame twins:** `track_frames_cursor` and `range_frames_cursor` go the same way, to `disc.frames_cursor(slot, span, CursorOptions::new())?` or `disc.track_frames(slot, n)?.into_cursor()` and `disc.range_frames(slot, first, end)?.into_cursor()`.
  - **The caller's index on a disc:** before, `disc.range_audio_cursor_indexed(slot, first, end, &index)?` and `disc.range_frames_cursor_indexed(slot, first, end, &index)?`. After, `disc.audio_cursor(slot, FrameSpan::Range(first, end), CursorOptions::new().with_index(&index))?` and the same with `frames_cursor`.
  - **Cursors without a disc,** on `TrackCursor` and `TrackAudioCursor` alike: before, `TrackCursor::for_range(&area, first, end, n)?`. After, `TrackCursor::open_range(&area, first, end, n, CursorOptions::new())?`.
  - **The same with an index:** before, `TrackCursor::for_range_indexed(&area, first, end, n, &index)?`. After, `TrackCursor::open_range(&area, first, end, n, CursorOptions::new().with_index(&index))?`.
  - **The same, provisional:** before, `TrackCursor::for_range_speculative(&area, first, end, n, Some(&index), options)?`. After, `TrackCursor::open_range(&area, first, end, n, CursorOptions::new().with_index(&index).with_policy(SeekPolicy::Speculative(options)))?`; for `None`, leave out `with_index`.
  - **Continuing:** before, `cursor.continue_to(&area, &track, n)?` and `cursor.continue_to_range(&area, first, end, n)?`. After, `disc.continue_track_audio(cursor, slot, number)?` and `disc.continue_range_audio(cursor, slot, first, end)?`, or `continue_track_frames` and `continue_range_frames` for a `TrackCursor`. There is no form without a disc.
