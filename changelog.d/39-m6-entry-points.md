Deprecated
- **One reading entry point per concept.** `Disc::{track_audio_cursor, range_audio_cursor, range_audio_cursor_indexed, track_frames_cursor, range_frames_cursor, range_frames_cursor_indexed}`, and `for_range`, `for_range_indexed`, `for_range_speculative`, `continue_to` and `continue_to_range` on `TrackCursor` and `TrackAudioCursor`, are deprecated in favour of `Disc::{audio_cursor, frames_cursor}`, `open_range` with the new `FrameSpan` and `CursorOptions`, and `Disc::continue_*`. They still work and give the same frames, errors and log lines, but **will be removed before 1.0, which will break callers then**. No C ABI changed (#39, #305). Migration: `docs/migrating-0.6.md`, M6.
