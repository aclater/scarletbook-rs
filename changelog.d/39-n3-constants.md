Deprecated
- `dst::MAX_CHANNELS` (a `usize`) and `dst::FL` repeat `audio::MAX_CHANNELS` (a `u8`) and `audio::FRAME_BYTES_PER_CHANNEL`, and are deprecated, to be removed before 1.0. Nothing breaks until then, except for builds that deny warnings. `scarletbook_pcm::MAX_CHANNELS` and `scarletbook_pcm::FRAME_BYTES_PER_CHANNEL` stay, because that crate does not depend on `scarletbook`. Before: `vec![0u8; dst::FL * dst::MAX_CHANNELS]`. After: `vec![0u8; audio::FRAME_BYTES_PER_CHANNEL * usize::from(audio::MAX_CHANNELS)]` (#39).
