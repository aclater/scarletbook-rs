Changed
- **Breaking:** six enums are now `#[non_exhaustive]`, so that later variants are not breaking changes: `audio::FrameStatus`, `audio::DecodeOn` (`parallel` feature), `SectorFormat`, `toc::LoudspeakerLayout`, `toc::Speaker` and `toc::TrackTextItem`. Outside the crate, a `match` on one of them needs a wildcard arm. The other exhaustive enums are now documented as frozen, each with its reason, and a new test holds them to it, so a `match` on them keeps working with no wildcard arm: `Slot`, `toc::AreaKind`, `toc::DiscTextItem`, `toc::AreaTextItem`, `audio::FrameKind`, `audio::FrameContent`, `audio::Stored`, `text::Charset` and `log::Level` (#39, #304). Migration: `docs/migrating-0.6.md`, M4.
