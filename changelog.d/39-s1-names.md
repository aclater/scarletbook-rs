Deprecated
- The names of provisional readings and the sector they start at now match the C interface's `SbSpeculation.claim`: on `AreaCursor`, `TrackCursor`, `TrackFrames`, `TrackAudioCursor` and `TrackAudio`, `speculation_id()` is renamed `claim()` and `counts_from()` is renamed `claim_lsn()`. The old names stay as deprecated methods, to be removed before 1.0; nothing breaks until then, except for builds that deny warnings. Before: `cursor.speculation_id()`, `cursor.counts_from()`. After: `cursor.claim()`, `cursor.claim_lsn()` (#39, #300).
- `Image::open_with_log`, `Image::with_format`, `Image::with_format_and_log`, `Disc::open_with_log`, `Disc::open_with_format` and `Disc::open_with_format_and_log` give way to one options value, `Image::options()` and `Disc::options()` (the new `ImageOptions` and `DiscOptions`). `open` and `share_with_log` stay. The old constructors are deprecated, to be removed before 1.0; nothing breaks until then, except for builds that deny warnings. Before: `Disc::open_with_format_and_log(reader, format, log)`. After: `Disc::options().format(format).log(log).open(reader)` (#39, #300).
