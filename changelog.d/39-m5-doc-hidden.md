Changed
- **Breaking for anyone relying on these items (hidden, still compiling):** FFI plumbing, tuning values and low-level text helpers are out of the semver promise: `#[doc(hidden)]`, each with a note that it may change or go in any release. No behaviour or C ABI changed, and calls still compile (#39, #297).
  - **`Continuation`:** `log_audio`, `audio_unlogged`, `audio_as`, `log_frames`, `frames_unlogged`, `frames_as`, `check_index`, `log_audio_indexed`, `audio_indexed_unlogged`, `audio_indexed_as`, `log_frames_indexed`, `frames_indexed_unlogged`, `frames_indexed_as`, `log_fresh_frames`, `fresh_frames`, and the enum `Opening` they use. Before: `let opening = next.log_audio(&prev); next.audio_as(prev, opening)`. After: `next.audio(prev)`.
  - **Tuning:** `dst::lanes`, `dst::MAX_LANES`, `audio::MAX_PARALLEL_BATCH`, `audio::PARALLEL_FRAMES_PER_THREAD`, `seek::MAX_CLAIMS`, `seek::MAX_EVENTS`, `seek::HINT_REACH` and `seek::REPLAY_SECTORS`. Before: `for each chunk of dst::lanes(channels) frames: decoder.decode_many(chunk, channels, outs)`. After: `decoder.decode_many(frames, channels, outs)`, which splits the frames into groups itself, with the same output; a caller who keeps grouping by `lanes` keeps its speed, without a promise. The constants have no replacement: don't size anything by them.
  - **Text:** `text::{decode, decode_string, fixed_bytes, fixed_text, language, string_bytes, text_string}`. Before: `text::text_string(buf, q, charset)` on bytes of the TOC. After: the decoded fields of `Toc`, `Area` and `Track` and their text. `text::Charset` and `text::MAX_STRING_BYTES` stay public.
