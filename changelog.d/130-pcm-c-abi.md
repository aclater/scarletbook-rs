Added
- C ABI for DSD to PCM (`scarletbook-pcm`): `sb_pcm_converter_new`, `sb_pcm_samples_per_frame`, `sb_pcm_convert_run` and `sb_pcm_converter_free` convert runs of plain DSD64 frames (4704 bytes, 37 632 samples per channel) to 24-bit PCM at 88.2, 176.4 or 352.8 kHz as `int32_t` (`SB_PCM_S32`) or 3-byte little-endian (`SB_PCM_S24LE`) samples, with the cutoffs `SB_PCM_CUTOFF_DEFAULT`, `_LOW` and `_WIDE`, a gain of -120 to +24 dB and per-channel clip counts. A frame converted with its two neighbours is byte for byte the same frame of a whole-area conversion. Additive on ABI 2; README section and ctypes bindings (#130, #PR).
