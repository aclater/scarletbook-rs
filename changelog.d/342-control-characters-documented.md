- Documented that ISRCs, catalogue numbers, language codes and disc, area and track text are returned as sacd-spec defines them and may contain control characters: ISRCs and catalogue numbers keep C0, DEL and C1 (specification 2, 2.6.5 item 4), language codes keep every character but trailing NUL and space (2.5.1 item 1), and text replaces C0 with a space but keeps U+007F and U+0080 to U+009F (2.6.4 item 3). Callers that print these values or put them in tags or file names should escape or filter them for that use. The stored-byte accessors are pointed to where they exist.
