Changed
- **Breaking, in small part:** `audio::Stored::Plain` is renamed `Stored::Dsd`, as in `FrameContent::Dsd`, `FrameKind::Dsd`, `FrameStatus::Dsd` and `SB_FRAME_KIND_DSD`; its meaning does not change. `Stored::Plain` stays as a deprecated constant, to be removed before 1.0, so expressions and patterns on a `Stored` value still compile; a pattern on a `&Stored` needs a `&` or a move to the new name. What breaks now: `use Stored::Plain` no longer compiles, `Stored::Plain` in a pattern through a reference, and `{:?}` of the value prints `Dsd`. Migration: `docs/migrating-0.6.md`, S1 (#39, #300).
