Deprecated
- `scarletbook-pcm` is deprecated: DSD-to-PCM conversion is no longer developed in scarletbook-rs, and its consumers convert on their own side. `Converter`, `PcmConfig` and `pack_s24le` carry `#[deprecated(since = "0.7.1")]`, and the C library's `sb_pcm_converter_new`, `sb_pcm_converter_free`, `sb_pcm_samples_per_frame` and `sb_pcm_convert_run` are declared with the new `SB_DEPRECATED(msg)` attribute macro of `scarletbook.h` (GCC, Clang and MSVC warn; define it as nothing to silence it). Both keep working as before and stay in the C ABI (`SB_ABI_VERSION` stays 2; `abi-check.py` ignores the attribute). They get no new features, only security and build fixes; removing them later would be a scarletbook-ffi ABI bump (#398, #PR).
