Changed
- **Breaking behaviour** in `scarletbook-pcm` (and so in the `sb_pcm_*` C functions): a neighbour of `None` (the edge of an area) is now the idle pattern 0x69, not the value 0, so it gives exactly the output of a neighbour frame of 0x69 bytes. The idle patterns 0x69 and 0x55 now convert to samples of exactly 0 at every rate, cutoff and gain, with no transient at the edges of an area. Samples near an area's edges, and all samples of silent passages, therefore differ from 0.5.2 (#171, #176).
- **Breaking behaviour** in `scarletbook-pcm`: `Low` now attenuates at least 105 dB from 36 kHz (was 38 kHz; -3 dB at 32.0 kHz, was 32.7 kHz). `Wide` at 176.4 kHz has its own filter, at least 105 dB from 88.2 kHz (was 96 kHz; -3 dB at 82.7 kHz, was 85.3 kHz). Every output of these filters differs from 0.5.2. `Cutoff::stopband_hz` of `Low` is 36 000, and the new `Cutoff::stopband_hz_at(rate)` gives the edge at a rate (#170, #176).
